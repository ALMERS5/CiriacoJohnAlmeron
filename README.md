# CJAlmeron Portfolio Website

This portfolio website serves as my personal and professional digital presence. It showcases my skills, projects, and experiences as a developer, while also acting as an online resume and creative space. The goal of this website is to give visitors such as recruiters, collaborators, and fellow developers a clear understanding of who I am, what I can build, and how to contact me.

# Purpose
*Present my technical skills and areas of interest
*Showcase selected projects with descriptions and technologies used
*Highlight my academic background, achievements, and experiences
*Provide an easy way for visitors to contact or connect with me
*Demonstrate my web development and design capabilities
